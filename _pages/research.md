I study how nonlinear systems produce complex behavior and how that behavior can be used for computation and design. My work spans modeling, simulations, and tabletop experiments.

## Transient dynamics and heteroclinic networks

At Constructor University Bremen, I study coupled heteroclinic networks as models of transient processes in the brain, including their relaxation and possible computational applications. See [On relaxation times of heteroclinic dynamics](https://doi.org/10.1063/5.0166803).

## Noise aided computation

During my PhD at IISER Mohali with Prof. Sudeshna Sinha, I studied how noise and nonlinearity interact in multistable systems to support logic operations. Later work explored synchronization and invertible logic in coupled nonlinear systems. See [Noise-Aided Invertible Logic from Coupled Nonlinear Systems](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevApplied.20.034041).

## Experimental nonlinear dynamics

At IIT Bombay with Prof. Punit Parmananda, I investigated nonlinear phenomena using tabletop experiments, including electronic circuits and coupled ethanol lamp oscillators. These experiments connect dynamical models to physical systems.

For a complete list of papers, see [publications](/publications/).
