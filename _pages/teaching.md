Research mentoring has been an important part of my work: helping students develop a question, connect models with experiments, and interpret what they find.

## From a student's idea to a research project

At IIT Bombay, I mentored **Vaibhav Pachaulee's MSc thesis** (January–July 2022). His idea of shuffling oscillator frequencies became a collaborative project on synchronization. I guided the research and experimental work, helped develop the project, and contributed to its presentation and writing. The resulting [Physical Review E paper](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevE.109.L052302) combines simulations, theory, and electronic oscillator experiments.

## Modelling and interpreting experimental observations

At FEMTO-ST, I contributed to mentoring **Zhenxing Hu** during part of his PhD research (2024–2025). I helped with the formulation and interpretation of a noisy bistable model of sleep onset, including understanding parameters inferred from EEG recordings. This collaboration led to our [PLOS Computational Biology paper](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pcbi.1014246).
